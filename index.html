SCP-8084 Exterior

Special Containment Procedures: An exclusion zone of 5km is to be maintained around the region where SCP-8084 is contained. Any non-anomalous individual/s discovered within the 5km exclusion zone are to apprehended and amnesticized by Mobile Task Force Gamma-5 ("Red Herrings") before being remanded into local law enforcement custody. In the event that an instance of SCP-8084-1 is to breach containment, Mobile Task Force Apollo-3 ("Game Wardens") is to handle containment or, if necessary termination efforts.

Personal entering SCP-8084 for researcher purposes are required to be escorted by PTF Alchothon-1 ("Rabbits in the Moon"), and may remain no longer than 6 hours per session.. Post successful capture, or termination, of any SCP-8084-A instances, senior researcher ███████ ██████ is to be notfied.

Description: SCP-8084 is an abandoned electrical station located 108km west of Washington D.C. within Harper's Ferry, West Virginia. Prior to SCP-8084 being brought to the Foundation's attention; it had stood abandoned by the United State's government for a period no less than 40 years. Due to SCP-8084's isolated location, and high risk of danger, it is possible that SCP-8084 had previously been rediscovered.


SCP-8084-1 refers to instances of humanoid entities found within the lower floors of SCP-8084. Instances of SCP-8084-1 display a high degree of agressivenes; with all documented encounters ending in violent attacks. Such attacks are not ill-concieved, with instances of SCP-8084-1 displaying complex hunting stratigies not unlike that of certain apex predators. Primary methods of attack typically are melee, however in rare cases SCP-8084-1 have displayed minor psionic capabilities.
